My research experience is rooted in algebraic topology and related areas of modern pure mathematics. I am especially interested in structural and compositional ways of thinking, and in how they can inform computational work.

## Master's dissertation

### Hopkins–Kuhn–Ravenel generalised character theory

**University of Oxford · MSc Mathematical Sciences · 2024**  
**Supervisor:** Dr Lukas Brantner

The dissertation concerned generalised character theory in chromatic homotopy theory, including complex-oriented cohomology theories and Morava E-theory. The project developed my ability to work independently with abstract mathematical literature and technically demanding arguments.

## Undergraduate research

### Loops and Covers

**University of Sheffield · Summer Undergraduate Research Experience · 2022**  
**Supervisor:** Professor Sarah Whitehouse

A funded six-week project in algebraic topology. I studied covering spaces and developed an approach to the Seifert–van Kampen theorem through the theory of covers.
