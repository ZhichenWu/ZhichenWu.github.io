Teaching has made communication central to the way I think about mathematics. Explaining a difficult idea requires more than presenting a correct solution: it requires identifying how a learner is reasoning, where that reasoning changes direction, and what the next useful hint should be.

## Mathematics teacher

**Suzhou, China · After completing my MSc**

- Teach A-Level Pure Mathematics and Statistics.
- Support preparation for UK mathematics competitions, including UKMT and BMO-style problem solving.
- Help students prepare for mathematically demanding university admissions, including Oxbridge-oriented work.
- Design explanations and feedback that emphasise reasoning, mathematical communication, and independent problem solving.

## Teaching and AI

My classroom experience motivates my interest in AI for mathematics education: systems that can interpret a learner's reasoning, locate misconceptions, and provide useful guidance without simply revealing an answer. This is a direction I am preparing to explore through future technical projects; it is not presented here as completed work.
